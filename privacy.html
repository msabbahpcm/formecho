FORMECHO · FORM FE-PRIVACY

Privacy policy

FormEcho is a Chrome extension that saves the answers you typed into a web form so it can put them back later. This page says exactly what it does with them.

Effective 26 November 2026

The short version

Everything you save stays on your own device. FormEcho has no server, makes no network requests, and its developer never receives your data.

What is saved

When you press Save answers, FormEcho stores, for each field you filled:

  • the value in the field;
  • a few facts used to find that field again: its name, label, placeholder and position;
  • the address of the page and the name you gave the saved form.

This is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

What is never saved

Password fields, payment card numbers, card security codes, one-time codes and hidden fields are skipped when you save and left untouched when you fill.

When FormEcho reads a page

Only when you press Save answers, Fill, or the keyboard shortcut on that tab. It does not run on pages in the background and has no access to your browsing history. The keyboard shortcut only fills a form that was saved on the same site.

Sharing and selling

None. FormEcho does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Backups

Back up writes your saved forms to a file in a place you choose. Anyone who has that file can read the answers in it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Removing your data

Delete a saved form in the FormEcho popup, or remove the extension from Chrome to delete everything it stored.

Permissions

  • storage: keeps your saved forms on the device.
  • activeTab and scripting: read and fill the form on the tab you are looking at, only after you ask.

FormEcho requests no access to other sites or tabs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text and its date will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions, or a form FormEcho does not handle: open an issue.
